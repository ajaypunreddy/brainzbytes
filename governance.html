Governance built into execution

Authority is explicit. Evidence is automatic.

Every protected human and workload action carries verified identity, tenant, purpose, policy version, correlation, and bounded authority. No prompt, model, Agent, tool, or generated package can expand its own privileges.

Identity

Who is acting?

User, workload, tenant, role, group, relationship, device, session, delegation, and current revocation state.

Policy

What is permitted?

Purpose, environment, region, data class, model, source, capability, operation, arguments, risk, and disclosure.

Evidence

What actually happened?

Decision IDs, plan and artifact hashes, release digests, approvals, citations, receipts, evaluations, metrics, logs, and traces.

Control model

Five layers around every consequential tool call.

Identity is resolved before the call, authority is checked at use time, credentials remain in the trusted gateway, and the result is minimized before returning to the Agent.

01

Authenticate

OIDC, MFA, workload identity, current tenant and subject context.

02

Inject identity

Bounded user, role, group, purpose, plan, node, delegation, and correlation context.

03

Broker credentials

Just-in-time scoped exchange. Agents never receive provider tokens, cloud keys, connector secrets, or database credentials.

04

Evaluate policy

Exact service, tool, operation, arguments, data, read/write risk, region, side effect, approval, and budget.

05

Validate and audit

Schema-check inputs and outputs; minimize fields; attach provenance; record receipt, decision, latency, and result class.

Non-negotiable safeguards

Human authority remains human.

These controls apply to platform engines and illustrate how ContextWeaver policy can encode customer-specific hard blocks.

Hard block

No autonomous high-severity acknowledgment

Severity 0, 1, and 2 incidents cannot be automatically acknowledged by an Agent.

Hard block

No autonomous merge or production rollback

Code merge and production rollback retain explicit independent human authority.

Hard block

No unreviewed sensitive communication or security change

Customer communications and authentication or cryptography changes require qualified review.

Separation of duty

No self-approval

The submitting or executing identity cannot approve its own restricted production action.

Promotion

Review before Enforce

Engines begin in advisory Review mode. Promotion requires passing metrics, at least two clean evaluation weeks, and human approval.

Auto-pause

Stop after repeated evaluation failure

Two consecutive evaluation failures automatically pause enforcement until review and recovery.

Lifecycle governance

Admission happens before deployment—and continues afterward.

Capabilities are not trusted forever because one build passed. Runtime identity, canary health, evaluation, drift, support, and lifecycle remain part of eligibility.

Validate the specification

Strict identities, owners, versions, schemas, side effects, idempotency, compensation, dependencies, credential and resilience profiles.

Inspect the supply chain

Trusted publisher, signatures, immutable digests, SBOM, dependencies, images, malware, secrets, licenses, provenance, and reproducibility.

Prove isolation and safety

Restricted runtime, deny-default network, bounded identity, resource quota, malformed I/O, adversarial, duplicate, retry, restart, timeout, and recovery tests.

Approve by risk

Data class, external authority, side effects, environment, tenant scope, residency, and human-authority requirements determine the decision path.

Qualify the live runtime

Compare the live descriptor to the signed release; use synthetic and canary traffic; continuously evaluate health, quality, safety, SLO, and drift.

Drain and retire safely

Block new resolution, preserve pinned work, revoke routes and authority, retain evidence, and remove resources according to retention policy.

Plain-language model

A guarded service counter—not a key handoff.

When an Agent needs an external system, the trusted gateway checks the exact request, uses a narrowly scoped credential on its behalf, and returns only the permitted result. The Agent never walks away holding the provider credential.
