Architecture without the document dump

Follow one request, then inspect the layer you own.

Begin with identity and admission, continue through graph compilation and authorization, then inspect durable execution, governed capability calls, state, evidence, and operations.

Open architecture explorer → Detailed call flows Source structure
AdmissionIdentity, intent, Service resolution, immutable versions.
CompilationTyped graph, compatible capabilities, exact data bindings.
AuthorizationEntitlements, policy, purpose, resources, budgets, side effects.
ExecutionQueues, workers, Agents, MCPs, RAG, approvals, retries.
EvidenceDecisions, citations, receipts, revisions, metrics, logs, traces.
Status language: “Implemented foundation” means the capability has working code and focused validation. “Active realization” means it is being expanded or hardened. “Target architecture” describes the intended production boundary. None of these labels claims automatic regulatory certification or guaranteed compliance.
