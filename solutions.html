Individual

Turn your own information and tools into reliable personal Services.

Begin with guided templates and human confirmation. Keep sources, memory, credentials, and outcomes bounded to the person and purpose.

Research companion

Register approved personal sources, retrieve with citations, compare evidence, and produce a traceable report.

Personal operations

Plan travel, track commitments, prepare documents, summarize projects, or coordinate tasks with explicit approval before side effects.

Developer copilot

Use code, documentation, repository context, test tools, and deployment workflows without exposing credentials to reasoning components.

Small business

Package how the business works, not just how one person prompts.

Shared Services convert repeatable know-how into accountable workflows with ownership, permissions, quality checks, and operational visibility.

Customer operations

Intake, classify, draft, approve, communicate, schedule, and preserve customer and service evidence.

Sales and finance

Research, quote, invoice, reconcile, collect approvals, and invoke payment or accounting tools through bounded authority.

Delivery and support

Turn requests into tasks, route specialists, track dependencies, manage exceptions, and produce a customer-ready outcome.

Enterprise

Operate a governed capability ecosystem across teams, clouds, and risk tiers.

Enterprises gain common contracts and evidence while preserving domain ownership, provider choice, data boundaries, and independent human authority.

Multi-tenant identity

Role, attribute, relationship, purpose, environment, and delegated authority carried end to end.

Risk-tiered lifecycle

Publisher trust, admission gates, independent approval, canary qualification, evaluation, drift, quarantine, and retirement.

Progressive isolation

Attributable operations
