The Problem with Today's Matching

❌ Traditional Platforms (Hinge, Bumble, LinkedIn)
  • Fixed parameters: age, location, height, job title — maybe 30–100 fields
  • Boolean matching: "likes dogs" YES/NO — no nuance
  • Can't express: "I need someone who understands immigrant family dynamics but isn't overly traditional"
  • Can't weight: "Ambition matters 10x more than height to me"
  • Everyone gets the same matching algorithm
✅ ContextWeaver Approach
  • Unlimited policies — each as descriptive as you want, in plain English
  • Semantic RAG matching — understands meaning, not just keywords
  • Hierarchical policies: dealbreakers → strong preferences → nice-to-haves
  • Both sides indexed — source policies match against destination policies
  • Privacy-first: your policies are never shown to the other person

How Policy-Based Matching Works

Each user creates policies at three tiers. Each policy is a plain-English document — as long and detailed as needed. ContextWeaver indexes them as RAG vectors and matches semantically.

Tier 1: Dealbreakers

Hard limits. If violated → instant reject.

"Must be within 50 miles"
"Non-smoker only"
"Must want children someday"
"No active substance abuse"
Tier 2: Strong Preferences

Heavily weighted. Missing these lowers the match score significantly.

"Values intellectual curiosity — reads regularly, enjoys learning new skills"
"Financially responsible — has savings goals, no reckless spending"
"Close to family but maintains healthy boundaries"
Tier 3: Nice-to-Haves

Bonus points. Additive — presence improves the score.

"Enjoys hiking, camping, outdoor adventures"
"Has a creative side — music, art, writing"
"Cooks at home, enjoys trying new cuisines"
"Has traveled internationally or wants to"

The power: Each policy can be a single sentence or a detailed paragraph. "I want someone who grew up with modest means and understands the value of hard work, but has the ambition to build something bigger — not someone born wealthy who's never had to struggle." Try expressing that with a checkbox.

What ContextWeaver Does

1
Index Both Profiles RAG Engine
index_policies(user="sarah", tier="dealbreaker", docs=12)
index_policies(user="alex", tier="dealbreaker", docs=9)

Each policy document is chunked, embedded as a 3072-dim vector, and stored in a per-user private index. Sarah's policies are never visible to Alex and vice versa.

2
Dealbreaker Cross-Check Policy Engine
cross_match(sarah.dealbreakers → alex.profile)
cross_match(alex.dealbreakers → sarah.profile)
