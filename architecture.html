Diagram semantics

Motion communicates meaning.

  • Solid pulse: active request or data
  • Dashed pulse: identity, policy, or authorization
  • Green: validated output or success
  • Amber: approval, waiting, retry, or compensation
  • Red: denial or failed validation
  • Parallel pulses: concurrent Agent work
