Product architecture

Three systems. One governed fabric.

Cerebro understands and governs. Weave composes user-facing Solutions from reusable Services, Workflows, Agents, MCPs, RAG profiles, models, and policies. Loom provides the portable Kubernetes, GitOps, data, messaging, identity, secrets, and observability foundation.

Cerebro

Think, govern, compile, orchestrate, prove.

The experience and control plane that turns an authenticated intent into an authorized immutable execution plan and durable outcome.

Weave

Compose reusable capabilities into Solutions.

The governed fabric of Solution definitions, Service contracts, Workflow graphs, Agents, MCP tools, data products, models, policies, and industry packs.

Loom

Run on a portable operational substrate.

Kubernetes, Helm, OCI, GitOps, databases, queues, object stores, search, identity, secrets, telemetry, and provider adapters.

Eight coordinated planes

Stable product semantics above replaceable infrastructure.

Cloud services implement platform contracts. They do not redefine identity, policy, Workflow, transaction, evidence, or lifecycle semantics.

The planes

Each layer has a precise responsibility and boundary.

Separation makes the system easier to operate, replace, scale, audit, and reason about than a single unrestricted “AI application.”

01

Experience

One authenticated entry point presents Builder/Admin Studio, Consumer My Solutions, or delegated headless access according to entitlement.

02

Governance

Identity context, entitlements, policy, admission, trust, approvals, audit, and separation of duties.

03

Registry & composition

Catalogs, schemas, alias resolution, compatibility, lifecycle, and immutable Workflow compilation.

04

Agents & MCP

Constrained reasoning, tool brokering, sandboxing, capability evaluation, and human authority.

05

Workflow runtime

Coordinator, task workers, queues, inbox/outbox, checkpoints, waits, Saga journal, and Finalizer.

06

RAG, memory & models

Governed sources, atomic generations, authorized retrieval, bounded memory, aliases, and providers.

07

Evidence & operations

Metrics, logs, traces, evaluations, cost, receipts, SLOs, alerts, and immutable audit export.

08

Infrastructure

Kubernetes, PostgreSQL, event bus, object storage, search, secrets, networking, and recovery.

Plain-language model

Think of the platform as a governed factory.

Cerebro is the control room, Weave is the approved parts catalog, and Loom is the factory floor. Models can suggest an assembly plan, but they cannot approve their own parts, issue themselves credentials, or bypass the safety gates.

Cloud neutral by contract

Deploy where your risk, cost, data, and sovereignty require.

The control plane uses portable specifications, OCI artifacts, Kubernetes APIs, Helm, GitOps, OpenTelemetry, and provider adapters.
