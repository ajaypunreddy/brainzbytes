Security and governance are runtime behavior

The model is never the authority.

AI can reason and propose. The platform determines what may run, which data and tools may be used, where human approval is required, how failure is handled, and what evidence must remain.

Identity

Authority stays attached

Every protected request carries verified user, tenant, role, group, and purpose context.

Policy

Exact graph decisions

Authorization covers the selected capability, resource, action, data, side effect, and current entitlement.

Data boundaries

Sensitive context stays constrained

Purpose, classification, source permissions, retrieval scope, redaction, retention, and egress rules travel with execution.

Credentials

AI never possesses secrets

Trusted gateways broker scoped runtime access; users and AI capabilities receive governed outcomes, not provider keys.

Human control

Accountability cannot self-approve

Risk-based gates, separation of duties, escalation, and approval policies preserve responsible decision authority.

Evidence

Every run can be examined

Decisions, grants, receipts, revisions, attempts, citations, metrics, logs, and traces remain reviewable.
