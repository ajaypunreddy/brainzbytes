Meet the Founder

👨‍💻

Ajay Punreddy

Founder & CEO, BrainzBytes

28+ Years in Tech Patent Holder 5 Patents

Ajay Punreddy is a veteran systems architect and engineering leader with 28+ years building mission-critical infrastructure across networking, telecom, cloud, and enterprise AI. He founded BrainzBytes to solve the fundamental security and orchestration gap in agentic AI — culminating in ContextWeaver, an enterprise platform for multi-user, policy-aware AI agents.

His career spans the full arc of modern networking and cloud infrastructure — from firmware at Cabletron Systems (pioneering Ethernet switching) and ATM/SONET at Bay Networks/Nortel & Synoptics, to frame-relay & ATM switching at Cascade Communications (acquired by Lucent). He was an early engineer at Castle Networks (acquired by Siemens), building carrier-grade VoIP with SIP, MGCP, and H.248 protocols, and a founding engineer at BlueNote Networks (acquired by Aspect Software), where he architected SIP/RTP-based unified communications from scratch.

At Aspect Software he rose to Technical Director / Software Architect, migrating enterprise call-center platforms to AWS cloud-native Kubernetes with full CI/CD, IAM (OAuth2/OIDC/SAML), and microservices architecture. He also contributed to Verivue (video streaming), Affirmed Networks (mobile core optimization), and other startups building high-performance network infrastructure.

Currently a Lead Software Engineer at Microsoft, Ajay brings deep expertise in Kubernetes, Azure, microservices, and platform engineering. With ContextWeaver, he combines decades of systems architecture experience with cutting-edge AI to create the first enterprise-grade, multi-user MCP orchestration platform — where every AI agent call is identity-aware, policy-enforced, and audit-logged.
