What ContextWeaver governs

Every layer needed to make AI operational.

ContextWeaver is not an Agent framework wrapped in a dashboard. It is the platform around Agents: how capabilities are defined, admitted, authorized, connected to knowledge, executed, paused, recovered, verified, and operated.

Services

Versioned business capabilities with intent, contracts, risk, budgets, authorization, Workflows, lifecycle state, and publication history.

Workflows

Reusable typed graphs with parallel branches, serial dependencies, artifacts, retries, human waits, verification, and compensation.

Agents and MCPs

Registry-discovered skills and tools with immutable versions, typed contracts, requirements, configuration, health, metrics, and dashboards.

Knowledge and RAG

Owned sources, ingestion generations, retrieval profiles, embedding aliases, access policies, freshness, verified citations, and evidence lineage.

Identity and policy

Whole-graph preflight, runtime reauthorization, capability tokens, credential isolation, budgets, human authority, and complete audit context.

Execution and operations

Durable acceptance, queues, replaceable workers, checkpoints, timers, HITL, Saga state, compact run history, metrics, logs, traces, and SLOs.

Prompt to verified result

The browser observes the run. It never owns it.

Once accepted, work continues independently of a web request or one pod. The durable execution contract allows another worker to resume safely after failures without losing the decision trail.

Design principles

Governance belongs in the platform, not the prompt.

The model may propose. The runtime decides what is allowed, what must wait, which credential reference may be used, and which evidence is required before the work becomes canonical.

Explore securityExplore NextGen architecture

Human authority

High-risk actions, approvals, external communication, merges, and rollbacks remain explicit human decisions.

Credential non-possession

Agents receive capability references and authority context, never raw secret values.

Replaceable workers

Execution state lives in durable stores, allowing another replica to recover accepted work.

Evidence-first results

Sources, citations, policy decisions, revisions, artifacts, and side-effect receipts stay attached to the run.
